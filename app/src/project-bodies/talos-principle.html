A tribute to Croteam.

The Talos Principle is one of my favorite games — a philosophical puzzler where ancient ruins and machine consciousness share the same sunlight. This self-initiated piece is an opening-title concept for it: type, pacing, and atmosphere composed the way a title sequence should breathe, letting the collision of stone and code set the tone before a single word of story is spoken.

Everything here — the visual concept, the motion language, and the final composite — is my own reading of the game's world, made purely out of love for it.

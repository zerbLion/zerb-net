LUNA OS is JMGO's in-house operating system for its projectors, released in 2021 as a dual-mode system: a classic viewing mode, and a "companion" mode that turns the idle projection into a living wall — an info dashboard, themed weather, a clock, music-reactive visuals and DIY surfaces, all guided by a borderless design that melts into the wall.

As Senior Motion Designer I led the motion design for the flagship O1 from R&D through market launch. My work covered the core information dashboard, the Dynamic Weather System, and the immersive 3D UI — plus explorations into naked-eye 3D and AR projection theming that pushed the interface from a flat 2D frame toward a spatial experience.

Beyond the OS itself, I directed high-quality concept promo videos for the launch, which meaningfully boosted market attention when the product shipped.

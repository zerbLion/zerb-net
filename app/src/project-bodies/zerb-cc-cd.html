MotionSheet is a tool I designed and built myself, born from a decade of watching the same handoff problem repeat: a designer polishes an animation in After Effects, and the developer receives… a video and a shrug. Easings get eyeballed, durations drift, and the shipped motion never quite matches the file.

MotionSheet takes an After Effects / Bodymovin (Lottie) export and turns it into a clean, developer-readable motion spec: a timeline of every layer, exact durations and delays, easing curves, and one-click copyable values. The designer keeps designing in AE; the developer gets numbers instead of guesses.

It's also the "Code" pillar of this portfolio in its purest form — the same design-to-engineering bridge I built for OS motion systems at vivo and JMGO, packaged as a web tool anyone can use. Designed, written and shipped end-to-end by me.
